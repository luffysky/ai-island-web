## Slide 0 — Open with a LIVE demo (no text, 90s)
Don't introduce yourself first — open the product on screen:
1. Home → open a lesson → **ask the AI tutor "Greenie" a question by pasting a screenshot of an error → streamed answer live.**
2. Show the note system (3-level knowledge tree) → **switch the whole page to English / Japanese / Korean instantly.**
3. Close with: **"This is a live product you can use right now — not slides."**

## Slide 1 — Who + one line (20s)
"I'm `[FILL name]`. **Solo founder + AI-assisted development.** I built **AI Island** — an AI-tutor-driven, gamified, job-oriented platform for learning programming and AI."

## Slide 2 — Problem (with data, 60s)
The core problem in online learning is **people don't finish.** *Science* (2019) analyzed 565 MIT/Harvard MOOCs: **completion rate is just 3.13%, and 52% of registrants never even start.** Meanwhile Taiwan faces a **severe software-talent shortage** (104: only 0.36 job-seekers per software opening), and **69% of 18–25-year-olds have used generative AI** (III/MIC 2025). Root cause: no instant help, no motivation, no companionship.

## Slide 3 — Solution / Product (live, 60s)
AI Island addresses all four gaps: **24/7 AI tutor (ask with screenshots) + gamification (3D island, pets, virtual economy) + community + a job pipeline (courses → practice → mock interview → certificate → résumé).** Scale: **80 chapters, 1,258 lessons, 4 languages, 392 APIs** — all in production.

## Slide 4 — Why me / Solo + AI efficiency (45s, your unique edge)
"This entire platform — 80 chapters of content, a multi-provider LLM engine, pgvector retrieval, a 4-language system — was built by **one person with AI assistance.** That means **extremely low labor cost and extremely fast iteration.** What usually takes a team, I did solo + AI."

## Slide 5 — Technical moat (30s, brief)
Not a bolted-on chatbot: a **self-built multi-provider LLM gateway** (6 providers, auto-failover, hard cost ceiling) + **RAG grounded on our own courseware** + **cross-conversation memory.** In one line: "AI assistants are now table stakes — but **no one else combines AI + deep gamification + UGC + zero-cost 4-language + a job pipeline** the way we do."

## Slide 6 — Market (30s)
Global EdTech reached **US$404B in 2025** (HolonIQ). We target the **Chinese-speaking programming / AI career-skills** segment. As AI spreads, "learn to use AI, learn to code" becomes a cross-generational necessity — and the heaviest AI users (youth) are our core audience.

## Slide 7 — Business model (45s)
Freemium: free is enough to start (incl. AI tutor); **Plus / Pro subscriptions** unlock premium models and career tools; plus token top-ups, a notes marketplace, and future **B2B / institutional licensing.** "AI cost has a **hard monthly ceiling**, and free users run on **zero-cost models** — cost is controlled and safe to scale."

## Slide 8 — Honest traction + plan (don't hide pre-launch, 45s)
"I haven't marketed yet — this is **early validation stage** (`[FILL user count]`). Over the next 3 months I'll run a **pilot: recruit ≥150 real learners, A/B the AI tutor and gamification, and push completion rate from the industry's ~3% toward 30%.** This prize / these resources are the fuel to start acquisition and validation."

## Slide 9 — The Ask (20s)
"I'm asking for **`[prize / space / mentorship / investor intros]`** to take a finished product to market, validate completion rate, and prove unit economics."
Closing line: **"The product is already built. What I need is to get it into the hands of the people who need it."**
